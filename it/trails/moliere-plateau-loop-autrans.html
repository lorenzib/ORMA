Molière plateau loop above Autrans

Vercors alpage loop to the Charande viewpoint

Terreno impegnativo Verificato da ORMA

An easy loop on the Molière plateau above Autrans, up to the Charande viewpoint over the Alps and back across the summer alpage past the Gîte de la Molière. The official page lists 5 km, 153 m up and down, about 1 h 30.

Da dove partire

La Molière car park at the end of the forest road, the board’s start

Come seguire questo percorso

Start at the La Molière car park, at the end of the forest road above the Feneys refuge.

Percorso: Head north towards the Col de la Croix Perrin, passing the Pas du Tracollet, on the yellow-and-red waymarks of the Tour des 4 Montagnes. At Charande, the highest point of Autrans at 1,709 m, stop for the view over the Alps, then carry on along the edge towards the Pas de l’Ours by Sous Charande. Leave the yellow-and-red waymarks and go down to the Gîte de la Molière, then cross the alpage, where heifers graze in summer, back to La Molière and past Sous le Tracollet to the car park.

Svolte principali: North at the car park for the Croix Perrin by the Pas du Tracollet; on at Charande towards the Pas de l’Ours; leave the yellow-and-red waymarks down to the Gîte de la Molière; across the alpage back by Sous le Tracollet.

Rifugi e ristoro

  • km 0,2 · Cabane sous le Tracollet
  • km 2,4 · Gite d'alpage de la Molière

Da sapere

Drive up from Autrans by the La Sure resort and the Feneys forest road to the La Molière car park: 5 km round, 153 m up and down, about 1 h 30. On summer weekends the road is closed from 10 am to 7 pm and a shuttle runs from La Sure; ask about snow outside summer.

Verificato da ORMA il 4 novembre 2026. Verifica le condizioni attuali prima di partire.

Questo sentiero fa per il tuo cane?

La valutazione qui sopra descrive la montagna ed è la stessa per ogni cane. Quanto sia adatto al tuo dipende da corporatura, età e salute. Vedi la compatibilità di questo percorso: è calcolata per un cane di taglia media finché non aggiungi il tuo, gratis e in un minuto.

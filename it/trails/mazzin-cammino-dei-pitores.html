Il cammino dei pitores

a loop from Mazzin into the Val di Dona

Fuori stagione Periodo migliore: da maggio a ottobre Stagione della processionaria Terreno impegnativo Verificato da ORMA

A loop from Mazzin up into the forest at the mouth of the Val di Dona and back through Fontanazzo and the woods by Campestrin, on the trail of the village’s travelling painters. The board lists 7.7 km, 248 m up and two and a half hours.

Da dove partire

The church of Mazzin, the board’s start

Come seguire questo percorso

Start in front of the church of Mazzin in the Val di Fassa, the board’s start.

Percorso: Take Strèda de Pian and, before the bridge over the Ruf de Udai, turn right up Strèda dò Ruf beside the stream. Cross the bridge on the left, follow the dirt road for about 150 m and turn right at a boulder with a red-and-white mark onto a lane that climbs through the woods, then levels. Cross a stream and, keeping right up a short ramp, leave the woods above the Solaria houses. Turn left onto the forest road that climbs beside the Ruf de Dona to the junction for the Val di Dona, then turn right on path 577 for about 700 m to Redolon. Keep right and go down past the Barcia Veia farm to Fontanazzo, then along Strada de l’Albolina to the public car park. Cross the Avisio bridge, pass the playground and follow the forest road through the woods towards Campestrin, past the PalaDolomites hall. Keep right at the fork, climb a short ramp to a clearing with benches, go down to a bridge and walk back into Mazzin up Strèda de Chiavacia and Strèda dò Ruf.

Svolte principali: Key turns: right up Strèda dò Ruf before the bridge; right at the marked boulder; left onto the forest road above the Solaria houses; right on path 577 at the Val di Dona junction; keep right at Redolon down to Fontanazzo; over the Avisio bridge; right at the fork near the PalaDolomites.

Da sapere

Start at the church of Mazzin: a 7.7 km loop, 248 m of climbing, about two and a half hours, as the board lists. The climb to the Val di Dona junction is steep in places, so the board suggests poles. Keep your dog on a lead of at most 1.5 m and carry a muzzle.

Verificato da ORMA il 4 novembre 2026. Verifica le condizioni attuali prima di partire.

Questo sentiero fa per il tuo cane?

La valutazione qui sopra descrive la montagna ed è la stessa per ogni cane. Quanto sia adatto al tuo dipende da corporatura, età e salute. Vedi la compatibilità di questo percorso: è calcolata per un cane di taglia media finché non aggiungi il tuo, gratis e in un minuto.

  1. Lead your dog away and stop any more chewing.
  2. Photograph the plant and where it grew. Note the time and how much.
  3. Call your vet or the nearest emergency clinic, even if your dog seems fine.

Do not induce vomiting or give food, milk or medicine unless a vet tells you to.

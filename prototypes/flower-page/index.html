Alpine plants guide: three redesign mockups

Each one replaces the pale-red “Think your dog ate an unknown plant?” banner and adds a different kind of interaction. All use the live plant catalogue.

  • A · Field guide: calm help strip, risk toggle, season and place chips, cards that open to show how to recognise each plant.
  • B · What’s out now: month picker with a small chart, place tiles, photo grid, a floating “My dog ate a plant” button.
  • C · Learn to spot them: step-by-step helper, “Safe or not?” quiz, flip cards.
  • Lean 1 · One filter row: short header, one-line help, one row of filters, cards grouped by risk.
  • Lean 2 · Simple list: no header photo, a plain list with small photos, an “In flower this month” switch.
  • Lean 3 · Danger first: only the 17 dangerous plants as photos; the other 16 folded away.

Did your dog chew a plant?

Move away, take photos and call your vet. Three steps, no panic.

  1. Move awayLead your dog away and stop any more chewing.
  2. Take photosThe whole plant, leaves, flowers or berries, and where it grew. Note the time and how much.
  3. Call your vetOr the nearest emergency clinic, even if your dog seems fine.

Don’t make your dog sick or give food, milk or medicine unless a vet tells you to.

Anello del Sole

Estimated: Caution Verification in progress · 26 Jul 2026 · 8/10 checks

A compact woodland loop from Niante Aze toward the marshy Lago d’Aosto, passing gypsum outcrops and mixed conifers before opening to a view toward the Spalti di Toro. The official route lists about 3 km and 250 m ascent.

At a glance (from the map data)

This is a true loop: it closes back on its own start point, so there's no return leg to plan; the 250 m of climbing is steady rather than steep, a fair ask for most fit dogs; no water points are mapped on this route, so carry everything your dog will drink.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Mapped route start at Niante Aze; verify current parking locally. Access suitability is not field reviewed.

Good to know

This route is an automated interpretation of OpenStreetMap data and has not been field reviewed by DoloPaws. Exposure, shade, livestock, seasonal access and current conditions remain unknown. Verify locally before setting out.

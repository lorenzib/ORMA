Hahnensee from Murtèl to St. Moritz

down past alpine lakes to St. Moritz Bad

Caution terrain Verified by ORMA

A long downhill walk from Murtèl, the Corvatsch middle station, past Lej da la Fuorcla to the Hahnensee restaurant by a moor lake, then through the forest past Lej Nair and Lej Marsch to St. Moritz Bad. The board lists 10.5 km, 115 m up and 1,042 m down, 3 h 11.

Where to start

Murtèl, the middle station of the Corvatsch cable car, the board’s start

How to follow this route

Start: Ride the Corvatsch cable car up from Surlej and start at Murtèl, the middle station, the board’s start.

Route: Take the path down to the small lake Lej da la Fuorcla. After a short climb the path meets the one to Fuorcla Surlej; from that junction follow the signs for Hahnensee. The path goes steeply down a scree slope and across moorland to the Hahnensee restaurant beside the moor lake Lej dals Chöds. From the restaurant go steeply down through the forest, first on a narrow path and then on a wide one. At the second junction leave the wide path to the left and walk past Lej Nair and Lej Marsch, then down Via San Gian and Via Mezdi into St. Moritz Bad, where the walk ends.

Key turns: Key turns: at the junction with the Fuorcla Surlej path, follow the signs for Hahnensee; below the restaurant, at the second junction, leave the wide path to the left for Lej Nair; then down Via Mezdi to St. Moritz Bad.

Good to know

Take the Corvatsch cable car up from Surlej to Murtèl and walk 10.5 km down to St. Moritz Bad, 115 m up and 1,042 m down, about three hours. Keep your dog on a lead. Drinking water at km 6.4, near the Hahnensee restaurant. Bus 601 links St. Moritz and Surlej for the way back.

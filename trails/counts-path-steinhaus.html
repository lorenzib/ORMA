Count’s path above Steinhaus

a short loop to the Unterberg hamlet

Out of season · back in April Best time: April to October Tick season Moderate terrain Verified by ORMA

A short loop above Steinhaus in the Ahrntal, up through the meadows to the Unterberg hamlet and back along the river to the Klausberg car park. The board lists 2.1 km and 86 m up.

Where to start

Klausberg car park in Steinhaus (Cadipietra), the board’s start

How to follow this route

Start at the Klausberg car park in Steinhaus (Cadipietra), the board’s start.

Route: Walk up the lane towards Enz and Schachen. Above the Enzhof farm, take the first path on the left to the Unterberg hamlet. Then go down and turn left along the river, which leads you back to the car park.

Key turns: Two turns: the first path on the left above the Enzhof, and left again along the river at the bottom.

Good to know

Start at the Klausberg car park in Steinhaus: a 2.1 km loop with about 90 m up and down, 45 minutes. Keep your dog on a lead of at most 1.5 m and carry a muzzle. Drinking water at km 0.5 and 1.5.

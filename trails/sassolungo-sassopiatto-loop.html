Sassolungo–Sassopiatto Loop

Caution Trail overview Paid access

A long, officially rated difficult circuit from Passo Sella via paths 557, 527 and 526, passing a chain of rifugi around the Sassolungo massif.

At a glance

This is a point-to-point route, so plan the return: retrace your steps or check local buses; 750 m of climbing makes this a big physical day, for conditioned dogs only, with turn-back discipline.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Passo Sella. Check current access before travelling.

Bring your own water

Do not rely on potential water locations being available or drinkable. Carry enough for the full walk.

Rifugi & refreshment

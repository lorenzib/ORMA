At a glance

This is a point-to-point route, so plan the return: retrace your steps or check local buses; 435 m of gain over 4.5 km is a real climb: budget rests, and halve expectations for short-legged dogs; no water points are mapped on this route, so carry everything your dog will drink.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Parking Col du Feu, 25 m from the route. Check current access before travelling.

Good to know

Use the live forecast, check local access information and be ready for changing mountain conditions.

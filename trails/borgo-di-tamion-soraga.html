Alla scoperta del borgo ladino di Tamion

the climb from Soraga to the hamlet of Tamion

Caution terrain Verified by ORMA

A steady climb from Soraga past the chapel of Barbide and above the red sandstone gorge to Tamion, a hamlet of old barns and a small church in the meadows, facing the Sassolungo. The APT lists 4.7 km and 339 m of ascent, there and back.

Where to start

Soraga di Fassa (Piaz Bepo Roch), the APT’s start

How to follow this route

This walk has no trail number of its own: follow these landmarks

Start in Soraga, at the tourist office, the tourist board’s start.

  1. From the tourist office of Soraga, go to the central square and take Strada del Cioch, then Strada de Barbida to the Barbide quarter.
  2. In front of the little church, turn right and straight away right again, under an archway. Past the arch, turn left and go straight on for about 150 m.
  3. Climb Strada de Parlaut past the Arnica Mountain Hotel, and at the top of the rise turn right. The way runs along the steep red sandstone cliffs, with the gorge below; the climb is hard, but the middle part is well shaded.
  4. Come out into small meadows that grow wider until you reach the level ground. A small rise on the left is a good viewpoint, over Soraga and Moena to the south and Tamion to the north. The lane winds up through the meadows.
  5. By an old hay barn you enter Tamion. Walk up to the centre of the hamlet and its little church. Come back the same way.

Directions: Val di Fassa tourist board, checked 29 October 2026.

Good to know

Start in the square in Soraga: 4.7 km there and back, about 340 m of climbing, two hours. Fill up at the fountains in Soraga, as there is no water higher up. Keep your dog on a short lead (1.5 m at most), carry a muzzle and keep out of the hay meadows. Take care coming down if the track is wet.

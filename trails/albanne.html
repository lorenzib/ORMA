Official Les Karellis route 09: a 7.7 km loop from Les Karellis to Albanne through forest and alpine pasture, following green waymarks. The official route lists 249 m ascent and about 3½ hours.

Good to know

Start at the Les Karellis tourist office and follow green route 09 toward Albanne. The route alternates shaded forest with open alpage; carry water even though mapped taps and springs appear near Albanne and Les Karellis.

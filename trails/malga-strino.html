Malga Strino

up the old military road in the Val Strino

Out of season · back in June Best time: June to October Cows on the pastures Caution terrain Verified by ORMA

An easy climb up an old First World War military road into the quiet Val Strino, from forest into pasture full of marmots, to Malga Strino and back. The board lists 6.7 km and 338 m up.

Where to start

The car park beside the SS42 at the mouth of the Val Strino, the board’s start

How to follow this route

This walk has no trail number of its own: follow these landmarks

Start at the small car park beside the SS42 at the mouth of the Val Strino, below Passo del Tonale, the board’s start.

  1. Car park on the SS42 at the mouth of the Val Strino
  2. Cross the SS42; old military road up through the forest
  3. Pastures along the stream
  4. Malga Strino (1,941 m); back the same way

Directions: APT Valli di Sole, Peio e Rabbi, checked 9 November 2026.

Good to know

Start at the small car park on the SS42 at the mouth of the Val Strino: 6.7 km and 338 m up, about 2½ hours there and back, as the board lists. An easy family walk on a gravel road; carry water and a muzzle.

At a glance

This is a true loop: it closes back on its own start point, so there's no return leg to plan; with only 52 m of climbing, it's one of the gentler options in the area for older dogs or hot days; no water points are mapped on this route, so carry everything your dog will drink.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Lift station Vizza, 86 m from the route. Check current access before travelling.

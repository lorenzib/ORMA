At a glance

This is a point-to-point route, so plan the return: retrace your steps or check local buses; the 359 m of climbing is steady rather than steep, a fair ask for most fit dogs; no water points are mapped on this route, so carry everything your dog will drink.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Parking, 22 m from the route. Check current access before travelling.

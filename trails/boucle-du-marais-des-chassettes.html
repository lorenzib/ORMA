Boucle du Marais des Chassettes

Low-risk DoloPaws route-audited

Photo: Anthony Levrot, CC BY-SA, via Wikimedia Commons (edited)

An easy hour around the Chassettes, a 22 hectare pocket of former peat bog squeezed between Challes-les-Eaux and La Ravoire at the foot of the Bauges. Woods and marsh alternate along the way, with information panels on a birdlife that ranges from black kites and honey buzzards to black woodpeckers, and the Challes leisure lake sits right by the start. Dogs are welcome on a leash per the Grand Chambéry tourism fiche.

Good to know

Keep the leash on, that's the condition for dogs here, and sensible anyway around ground-nesting birds. A treat for local dogs: two fenced dog parks sit by the leisure lake at the start, one for training and one for free running. Flat, short and close to town, a good pick for seniors, puppies or a quick evening lap. No fountains are mapped, so bring water on warm days.

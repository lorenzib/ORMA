At a glance

This is a true loop: it closes back on its own start point, so there's no return leg to plan; the 184 m of climbing is steady rather than steep, a fair ask for most fit dogs; no water points are mapped on this route, so carry everything your dog will drink.

Where to start

Mapped start suggestion — Lift station Rotwand - Croda Rossa, 12 m from the route. Check current access before travelling.

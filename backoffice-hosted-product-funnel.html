Measurement boundary

Useful without becoming invasive

Only customers who explicitly enable anonymous product usage are counted. ORMA stores coarse hourly timestamps and bounded categories—not dog names, searches, precise locations, health notes, photos, or raw GPS traces. This page shows aggregates and never exposes browser identifiers.

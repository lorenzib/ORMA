Safety library

Mountain conditions can change quickly, and the same route can affect every dog differently. Use this library to plan for heat, altitude, water, terrain and shared spaces; recognise early warning signs; and know when to turn back or seek help. Each guide turns common mountain risks into practical decisions you can make before you leave and while you are on the trail.

This library provides general information, not veterinary advice. If your dog shows signs of heat stress, injury or exhaustion, stop early and seek professional help when symptoms do not resolve promptly.

Five quick checks

Are you ready for the trail?

Answer for the dog, route and conditions you are planning today.

1. Have you checked today’s forecast, trail status and heat exposure?
2. Are you carrying enough water for both you and your dog?
3. Do you have a leash, muzzle where required and a basic first-aid kit?
4. Is the distance, climb and terrain appropriate for your dog today?
5. Have you saved the route and local emergency contacts for offline use?

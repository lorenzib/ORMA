# DATA-04 — Positioned route segment advisories

**Status:** Contract complete in code; trail data population is outstanding
(2026-09-04)

**Extends:** [`DATA-01`](DATA-01-canonical-trail-schema.md)

## Decision

`DATA-01` described a route with whole-route attributes and point waypoints.
Neither can express the most useful sentence ORMA can say to an owner:

> Lead recommended between kilometres 2.1 and 3.4.

A waypoint is a place. A whole-route flag is a mood. An advisory needs a
*stretch*. Schema version `1.1.0` adds `segments`, a required array on every
record, and five behaviour attributes to `suitability`.

## Segment shape

```json
{
  "id": "upper-pasture",
  "type": "livestock",
  "fromKm": 2.1,
  "toKm": 3.4,
  "advisory": "leash-recommended",
  "note": "Open grazing pasture with free-roaming cattle from June to September",
  "status": "reviewed",
  "seasonal": true
}
```

- `type`: `livestock`, `wildlife`, `road`, `exposure`, `crowding`, `surface`,
  `water-scarce`, `other`.
- `advisory`: `leash-required`, `leash-recommended`, `avoid`, `caution`,
  `information`.
- `status`: `reviewed`, `mapped`, `reported`, `unknown` — a community report
  and a field review are not the same claim.

`fromKm` and `toKm` are required and ordered. An advisory that cannot be
placed on the route is rejected by the validator and dropped by the scorer,
because "lead on somewhere" is less useful than saying nothing at all.

An empty `segments` array states that no advisory is recorded. It does not
claim that none exists.

## New suitability attributes

| Field | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `livestockPresence` | `none`, `seasonal`, `likely`, `unknown` |
| `wildlifePresence` | `low`, `moderate`, `high`, `unknown` |
| `sightlines` | `open`, `mixed`, `restricted`, `unknown` |
| `roadProximity` | `none`, `crossings`, `alongside`, `unknown` |
| `crowding` | `quiet`, `moderate`, `busy`, `unknown` |

These carry the score. Segments carry the *where*. Keeping them separate is
what stops one grazing pasture being charged to the score twice, once as a
route attribute and once as an advisory.

`livestock` already existed as a review *category* in `verification`. That
records whether the evidence was reviewed, not whether livestock are there.
The two are now distinct.

## Unknown is still data

Each new attribute defaults to `unknown`, and the `DATA-01` rule applies
unchanged: every `unknown` must be declared as a JSON Pointer in
`unknownFields`. `scripts/validate-trail-schema.js` enforces it, and it caught
the imported example during this change.

`scripts/trail-adapter.js` therefore emits `unknown` for every legacy record
and never infers presence from prose. A description that mentions cattle is
evidence of a sentence, not of grazing on the day someone walks the route.

## Migration

Canonical records are generated by `trail-adapter.js` rather than stored, so
adding a required field touched six pins and no production data:

- `schemas/trail.schema.json` — `schemaVersion` const `1.1.0`;
- `scripts/trail-schema.js` — domain validator;
- `scripts/trail-adapter.js` — emits `1.1.0`, `ADAPTER_VERSION` `1.1.0`;
- `scripts/validate-production-trails.js` — report version;
- both records in `data/examples/`.

All 165 production trails adapt and validate against `1.1.0`; 143 publish and
22 remain drafts for pre-existing geometry and distance reasons.

## Outstanding

No production trail declares a behaviour attribute or a segment yet — every
one is `unknown` with `segments: []`, which is the honest state. Populating
them is editorial work under the existing review desks, and the `livestock`
review category is the natural place to start. Until then the `SCORE-03`
rules are correct and inert on live data.

## Verification

`trail-schema.test.js` covers the segment shape, rejection of an unorderable
or unplaceable range, rejection of an unrecognised advisory or type, and the
undeclared-unknown rule for the new attributes.

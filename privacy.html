Privacy

What we store, and why

Plain language, no surprises. Last updated 24 July 2026.

The short version

You can browse every trail without an account, and without us storing anything about you beyond ordinary hosting logs. If you create an account, we store what you give us — your email and your dog's profile — so your trails follow you across devices. We don't run ads, we don't sell data, and community posts are the only thing other people ever see.

What we store when you have an account

  • Your login — your email address (or your Google account, if you sign in with Google). Authentication is handled by Google Firebase; we never see or store your password.
  • Your dog's profile — name, breed, age, weight, fitness and any health notes you add, plus an optional photo. This exists purely to score trails for your dog.
  • Your activity — saved trails, journal entries, and your latest trail matches.
  • Community posts — reviews, condition reports and trail photos you submit for moderation. Approved posts become public and appear with your dog's first name (“Rufus's human”), never your email.

All of this lives in Google Firebase (Firestore), in your account's own record.

What stays on your device

We use your browser's local storage for convenience caches: your language choice, a summary of your dog's profile so pages load personalised without a round-trip, your dog's photo, and which “what's new” notes you've seen. It never leaves your browser, and logging out clears the account-related parts.

Anonymous counters

When someone starts hike mode on a trail, we count it — trail id and timestamp, nothing else. No account, no location, no device identifiers. It powers the “dogs hiked this week” number.

Anonymous product usage is separate and optional. It is off until you enable Share anonymous product usage in Settings. Allowed events use a random browser identifier, hour-level timestamps and predefined categories; they reject names, email addresses, search text, reviews, health notes, exact coordinates and GPS history. Events waiting offline expire after 30 days. Switching the setting off deletes that queue and its identifier immediately.

Third parties

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database (see above).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the site; like any web host it keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap / OpenMapTiles — your browser fetches fonts and map tiles from their servers when pages and maps load.

We run no advertising or third-party tracking scripts.

Deleting your data

Open your account page and choose Cancel account. That deletes your account record — profile, saved trails, journal — and your login itself. If anything lingers or you can't log in to do it, email hello@dolopaws.com and we'll remove it by hand.

Questions

Anything unclear, or a request this page doesn't cover: hello@dolopaws.com.
